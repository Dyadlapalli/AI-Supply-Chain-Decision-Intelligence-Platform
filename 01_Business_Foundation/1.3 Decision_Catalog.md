# Decision Catalog

At its core, this platform is designed to help people make better decisions.

Most supply chain teams already have reports. The challenge is not a lack of information, it's knowing what action to take when something needs attention.

This document outlines the key decisions the platform is expected to support and why each one matters to the business.

---

# Decision 1: Should We Reorder Inventory?

One of the most common challenges in supply chain operations is ensuring inventory is available before customers need it.

If demand is growing faster than expected or inventory levels are declining, planners need to decide whether additional inventory should be purchased.

### Questions We Need To Answer

- Do we have enough inventory to meet upcoming demand?
- How long will current inventory last?
- When will we run out if demand continues at the current pace?
- How much additional inventory should be ordered?

### Desired Outcome

Replenish inventory before shortages impact customers or revenue.

---

# Decision 2: Should Inventory Be Transferred Between Branches?

It is common for one location to have excess inventory while another location is facing a shortage.

Before purchasing additional inventory, the business should determine whether inventory already exists elsewhere in the network.

### Questions We Need To Answer

- Which branches have excess inventory?
- Which branches are experiencing shortages?
- Can inventory be moved internally?
- What is the most cost-effective option?

### Desired Outcome

Use existing inventory whenever possible before spending money on new purchases.

---

# Decision 3: Should A Purchase Order Be Expedited?

There are situations where inventory may run out before a supplier shipment is expected to arrive.

In these cases, supply chain teams must determine whether a purchase order should be expedited.

### Questions We Need To Answer

- Will inventory run out before the shipment arrives?
- How many customers could be impacted?
- What is the cost of expediting versus waiting?

### Desired Outcome

Prevent avoidable stockouts and maintain customer service levels.

---

# Decision 4: Does A Supplier Require Attention?

Supplier performance has a direct impact on inventory availability and customer satisfaction.

When suppliers consistently deliver late or fail to meet expectations, the business should identify the issue early.

### Questions We Need To Answer

- Which suppliers are missing delivery commitments?
- Are lead times increasing?
- Which suppliers represent the largest risk to operations?

### Desired Outcome

Improve supplier accountability and reduce operational risk.

---

# Decision 5: Is Inventory Becoming Excessive?

Holding too much inventory can be just as costly as having too little.

The business should identify inventory that is no longer aligned with expected demand.

### Questions We Need To Answer

- Which parts have more inventory than necessary?
- How much working capital is tied up in excess inventory?
- Should inventory be reduced, transferred, or purchasing paused?

### Desired Outcome

Optimize inventory investment while maintaining service levels.

---

# Decision 6: Can The Data Be Trusted?

Good decisions depend on good data.

Before stakeholders act on recommendations, they need confidence that the underlying information is accurate and complete.

### Questions We Need To Answer

- Are there data quality issues affecting decisions?
- Which source systems require attention?
- What records failed validation?

### Desired Outcome

Ensure decisions are made using reliable and trusted data.

---

# Decision 7: What Should Leadership Focus On Right Now?

Leaders rarely have time to review every report and every issue.

The most valuable capability of this platform is helping leadership focus on the issues that matter most.

### Questions We Need To Answer

- What are the most important risks today?
- What actions require immediate attention?
- What opportunities will create the biggest business impact?

### Desired Outcome

Provide a prioritized list of actions that helps leadership focus on the highest-value decisions.

---

# Success Criteria

This platform will be successful if users can:

1. Identify risks before they become major problems
2. Understand why those risks exist
3. Evaluate available options
4. Make informed decisions
5. Measure the impact of those decisions over time

Ultimately, the goal is not to build another dashboard.

The goal is to help supply chain teams spend less time searching for information and more time making confident decisions.
