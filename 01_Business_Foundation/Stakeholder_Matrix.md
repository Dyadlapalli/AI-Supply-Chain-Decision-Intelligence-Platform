# Business Problem Statement

## Project Name

AI-Powered Supply Chain Decision Intelligence Platform

## Industry Context

Organizations operating large distribution networks and dealer supply chains manage thousands of parts across multiple branches, suppliers, and equipment lines. Maintaining the right inventory levels while meeting customer demand is a constant challenge.

Too little inventory can lead to stockouts, delayed customer deliveries, and lost revenue. Too much inventory ties up working capital and increases holding costs. Supplier delays, demand fluctuations, and poor data quality make these decisions even more difficult.

## Business Challenge

Most supply chain teams have access to reports that explain what happened in the past. However, decision makers often struggle to answer critical questions such as:

- Which parts are likely to stock out?
- Which branches have excess inventory?
- Which suppliers are creating operational risk?
- What is the financial impact of these risks?
- What actions should be taken today to prevent future issues?

As a result, teams spend significant time manually analyzing reports, investigating root causes, and determining corrective actions.

## Project Vision

The goal of this project is to transform traditional reporting into a decision intelligence platform that helps supply chain leaders move from reactive decision making to proactive planning.

Instead of simply displaying historical metrics, the platform will:

- Detect operational risks before they occur
- Explain the drivers behind those risks
- Simulate potential actions and outcomes
- Recommend the most appropriate next steps
- Support faster and more informed business decisions

## Target Users

The platform is designed for:

- Executive Leadership
- Supply Chain Leaders
- Procurement Managers
- Inventory Planners
- Branch Operations Managers
- Supply Chain Analysts
- Data Stewards

## Expected Business Outcomes

By providing actionable insights and guided recommendations, the platform aims to:

- Reduce inventory stockouts
- Improve inventory utilization
- Lower excess inventory levels
- Increase supplier performance visibility
- Improve trust in operational data
- Accelerate decision making across the supply chain

## Success Statement

The platform will be successful if users can quickly identify risks, understand their causes, evaluate available options, and confidently make data-driven decisions that improve service levels, reduce costs, and protect revenue.
