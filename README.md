# AI-Supply-Chain-Decision-Intelligence-Platform
AI-Powered Supply Chain Decision Intelligence Platform for inventory optimization, forecasting, supplier risk analysis, and executive decision support.

## Project Highlights

### Case Study: The same supplier under different names

The supplier master holds **46 records for 36 real suppliers**. Ten suppliers exist twice under names like `Cat Parts Dist Ctr - Atlanta` vs. `Caterpillar Inc. - Parts Distribution Center Atlanta`, or differ only by an invisible trailing space. **$317,855 of purchase order spend** is booked to the duplicate IDs, splitting every supplier KPI and misleading supplier risk analysis.
